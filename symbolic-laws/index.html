Symbolic Physical Laws Discovery

An open course on discovering physical laws with computers: how symbolic regression searches for equations, how physics knowledge guides it, and how to tell a fitted formula from a law. 10 modules, one per chapter of Teddy Lazebnik's forthcoming book, with long videos, slides and browser labs.

The course book. Teddy Lazebnik, Computational Methods for Symbolic Physical Laws Discovery. Coming soon. Each module follows one chapter of the book; the videos, slides and labs are written in our own words, figures and simulations.

How to study each module

Every module page brings the same three things together. Use them in any order; this one works well.

Watch

Two or three animated videos per module, 8 to 15 minutes each, drawn like a blueprint as they go, with a transcript to read along. Each has a pause-and-predict moment and a worked example with our own numbers.

Read the slides

Each deck is written to be read on your own: worked examples, our own small simulations, common pitfalls, and check-yourself questions with answers at the end. The matching chapter of the book goes deeper.

Try it

Every module has a lab that runs in your browser, starting with curve versus law in Module 1. The challenges are checked as you go.

Labs

The labs run the course’s ideas in your browser, with challenges that are checked as you go. One lab per module; new ones appear as modules go online.

Curve versus law

A polynomial and two drag laws on falling-ball data; Kepler's law with Uranus held out.

Module 1 lab

Expression workbench

Trees, prefix and postfix, sharing, units and equivalence for formulas you type.

Module 2 lab

Units guard

Units, dimensionless groups, soft and hard rules, and a ladder of knowledge for a draining tank.

Module 3 lab

Search arena

Random search, enumeration and genetic programming race for the range law on one budget.

Module 4 lab

Noisy derivatives

Finite differences, smoothing and weak forms on noisy video of a spring, and the next swing to record.

Module 5 lab

Collapse and envelope

Collapse nine pendulums onto one curve, break it with a missing variable, and measure a formula's validity envelope.

Module 6 lab

Sparse dynamics

Find a chemical clock's law with sparse regression, and test it on steady states, the onset of ticking and a feed nobody ran.

Module 7 lab

Field discovery

Find a spreading plant's field law from noisy sensors, and test it on two releases that merge.

Module 8 lab

Stress-test bench

Stress-test a tank law: calibration, controls, and the experiment that falsifies its rivals.

Module 9 lab

Distil a black box

Distil a black box into a formula, and test it beyond the data.

Module 10 lab

Advancing computational mathematics through real-world problem solving.
