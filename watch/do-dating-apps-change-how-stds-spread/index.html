Do dating apps change how STDs spread?

We extended the SIS epidemic model, in which people can be infected again after recovering, to include dating app users, and ran simulations based on real-world data, a hundred runs per setting. The more people adopted dating apps at the start, the higher the reproduction number climbed, and left unmanaged, wider adoption could push a population into an outbreak. Limiting users' interactions, or asking for regular infection-free test results, can help mitigate the extra spread. A simulation model, not measured data.

Based on the paper
