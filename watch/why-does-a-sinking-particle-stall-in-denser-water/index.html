Why does a sinking particle stall in denser water?

We tracked many particles settling through layers of different density, and machine learning searched our data, together with an earlier experiment's, for what controls the delay time: how long a particle takes, after leaving the boundary layer, to reach a steady speed. It depended strongly on six dimensionless numbers; the Reynolds and Froude numbers were correlated in our experiments, and the best symbolic-regression formula uses the Froude number alone. Trajectories fell into three types. The formula holds within the range of these experiments.

Based on the paper

Improved prediction of settling behavior of solid particles through machine learning analysis of experimental retention time data

International Journal of Multiphase Flow, 2024
