Do scientists forage like birds?

Explore a new venue or return to a familiar one? Across about 650,000 computer scientists and 18 million papers, about three in four publish in a pattern that fits explore-or-exploit, with a “home venue” that typically emerges after 15 to 20 papers.

Based on the paper

Publishing instincts: An exploration-exploitation framework for studying academic publishing behavior and 'Home Venues'

Journal of Informetrics, 2025
