Let equations design your model's features

Symbolic regression searches for formulas that fit the data; we used the formulas it finds as new features for machine learning and deep learning models. On synthetic data the error (RMSE) dropped by 34 to 86%, and on 21 real-world datasets by 4 to 11.5%, with both kinds of model improving significantly. Predicting superconducting critical temperatures based on Eliashberg theory, the error fell by more than 20%, and the formula features are readable. The gains were much larger on synthetic data.

Based on the paper

Symbolic regression as a feature engineering method for machine and deep learning regression tasks

Machine Learning: Science and Technology, 2024
