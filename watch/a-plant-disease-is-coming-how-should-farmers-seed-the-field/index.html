EpidemiologyShort · 1:06Coming 31 January 2027

A plant disease is coming. How should farmers seed the field?

Farmers can't stop a plant pathogen, but they can choose where to put each plant. We joined a spatial SIR model of the plants with a non-linear economic model of the field's output, and built an algorithm that finds the most profitable grid pattern for seeding. The strategy brought a significant economic benefit; the recovery rate and the basic infection rate mattered about equally for profit, and a bigger farm did not promise a bigger profit. A mathematical model with simulated fields, not a field trial.

Based on the paper

Cost-optimal Seeding Strategy During a Botanical Pandemic in Domesticated Fields

Chaos, 2024
