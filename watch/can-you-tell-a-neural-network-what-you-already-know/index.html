Can you tell a neural network what you already know?

Autoencoders compress data into a code that other models use, but developers can't easily shape it, so samples that experts know belong together may end up far apart. Our Knowledge-integrated AutoEncoder, KiAE, brings external knowledge into training and keeps the desired distances and neighborhoods between groups of samples, even when the knowledge is only partly known. On three large datasets from economics, physics and biology, against nine other models, it gave a more useful representation and the best reconstruction accuracy. Wrong knowledge hurts it.
