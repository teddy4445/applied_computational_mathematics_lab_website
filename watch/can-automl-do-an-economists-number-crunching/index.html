Can AutoML do an economist's number crunching?

We compared two biology-inspired AutoML tools, two other AutoML tools and two traditional methods on 50 real economic datasets, each confirmed as economic by three independent economists. Every AutoML tool beat the traditional methods on 73% of the datasets, and at least one did on 87%. On held-out test data the bio-inspired tools reached R² = 0.72, the other AutoML tools 0.70 and the traditional methods 0.58. Mostly regression tasks, with no data preprocessing.

Based on the paper

Benchmarking Biologically-Inspired Automatic Machine Learning for Economic Tasks

Sustainability, 2023
