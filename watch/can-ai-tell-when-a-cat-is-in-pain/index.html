Can AI tell when a cat is in pain?

Our system finds a cat's face in ordinary video and tracks 48 facial landmarks placed by the muscles underneath, with no hand-picked photos. Reading six frames a second, it judged pain correctly 70% of the time for cats filmed before and after surgery and 66% for cats in a veterinary clinic, against 65% and 51% for single frames with the same automated landmarks: movement matters. The datasets were small and a model trained on one did poorly on the other, so this is a research step, not a clinical tool.

Based on the paper

Automated Video-Based Pain Recognition in Cats using Facial Landmarks

Scientific Reports, 2024
