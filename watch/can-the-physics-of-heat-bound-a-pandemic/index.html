Can the physics of heat bound a pandemic?

Models like SIR need infection and recovery rates that take time to measure, so we treated a pandemic on a social network like heat spreading through a material, which gives a worst-case and a mean-case bound. On a real social network of about 4,000 people, the worst-case bound ran about 20 times faster than the simulated epidemic and the mean-case bound about 1.7 times. Early on, use the bounds; once data arrive, switch to a full SIR model.

Based on the paper

Bounding Pandemic Spread By Heat Spread

Journal of Engineering Mathematics, 2023
