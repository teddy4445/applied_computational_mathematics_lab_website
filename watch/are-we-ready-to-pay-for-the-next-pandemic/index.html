Are we ready to pay for the next pandemic?

We propose a small dedicated income tax that fills a reserve budget, and a reserve program that moves people out of work in non-essential jobs into essential ones during a crisis. In a hundred-year simulation based on Israeli data, with an outbreak every 16 to 18 years, a tax of about 3% of the existing income tax and reserve pay of 80% of salary prevented a prolonged crisis, kept the budget out of deficit and kept output relatively high. A simplified economy.

Based on the paper

Financing and managing epidemiological-economic crises: Are we ready for another outbreak?

Journal of Policy Modeling, 2023
