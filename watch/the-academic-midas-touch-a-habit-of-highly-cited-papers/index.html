The Academic Midas Touch: a habit of highly cited papers

Instead of totals like the h-index, the Academic Midas Touch measures the share of a scientist's papers that become highly cited soon after publication (here, at least 15 citations within three years). Across 8,468 mathematicians it correlated with popular metrics without fully aligning with them. Winners of the Fields Medal, Abel Prize or Wolf Prize had a higher Midas Touch than matched peers, while their h-index and citation counts showed no significant difference. It focuses on short-term citations.

Based on the paper

The Academic Midas Touch: A citation-based indicator of research excellence

Journal of Data and Information Science, 2025
