Does a busy coat make a cat harder to read?

In primates, busier face colours go with fewer facial movements. We filmed 53 cats at a cat café, coded 688 facial signals with catFACS and scored each face pattern with AI, and found no detectable link between facial colour pattern and expressiveness.

Based on the paper

Facial color patterns and intraspecific facial signaling complexity in domesticated cats (Felis silvestris catus)

Applied Animal Behaviour Science, 2026
