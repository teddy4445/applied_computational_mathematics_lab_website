What if equation-finding AI could see the neighbors?

Symbolic regression searches for a formula that explains data, but usually treats every row of a table as independent, while in physics a point depends on its neighbors in space and time. We add a second representation, a graph whose nodes are places and moments, turned into extra features by a graph neural network that any symbolic regression tool can use unchanged. Across ten tools and ten physics equations, results improved or matched for every tool, mostly by 1 to 3%, and errors grew up to 10 to 15% more slowly with noisy data. Tested on synthetic benchmarks.

Based on the paper

Moving from table to graph in physics-informed spatio-temporal symbolic regression

Scientific Reports, 2026
