AI detectors flagged papers written before the AI boom

We tested four detectors of AI-generated text on 11 papers that openly credited ChatGPT, each paired with an earlier paper by the same lead author: they were right only 55–64% of the time. On 1,094 papers from 2022 or earlier they still flagged 8.8–17.2% as AI-assisted, up to about 1 in 6. A simple detector of sudden writing-style jumps did better (73% accuracy, 3.1% false alarms), though with only 11 known pairs the accuracy gap wasn't significant.

Based on the paper

Detecting LLM-assisted writing in scientific communication: Are we there yet?

Journal of Data and Information Science, 2024
