What does an acidifying ocean do to sharks?

We screened over a thousand papers and analysed 58 experiments, adding bony fish because data on sharks is scarce. Most tested the acidity expected by 2100 (pH about 7.8), and 79% of the fish experiments found harmful effects of higher carbon dioxide. In sharks it dulled senses such as smell and changed hunting and swimming, though sharks showed some resilience and effects varied between species. Every shark study was on bottom-dwelling species and most were short, so the long-term impact is still unknown.

Based on the paper

A Systematic Review of the Behavioural Changes and Physiological Adjustments of Elasmobranchs and Teleost’s to Ocean Acidification with a Focus on Sharks

Fishes, 2022
