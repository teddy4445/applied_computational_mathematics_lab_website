ICU feeding: follow the body's clock, not the calendar

Critical illness puts the body in a breakdown state, and recovery begins when it switches to rebuilding, but there's no validated marker of that switch. We built one from a daily insulin resistance index. In 2,350 ICU patients, 94% made the switch, about 60% by day three; switching by day three was linked to lower 90-day mortality (hazard ratio 0.72). Heavy feeding before the switch was linked to higher mortality (odds ratio 1.25), while heavy feeding timed by calendar days showed no such pattern. One hospital's records; associations that need validation.

Based on the paper

Detecting the metabolic transition to personalize nutritional timing: model development and preliminary validation in a large ICU cohort

Critical Care, 2026
