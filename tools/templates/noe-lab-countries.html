The true hidden share depends on taxes, enforcement, trust, self-employment and digital payments, plus each country's quirks. A published estimate leans on cash use, so it runs high where cash is common and low where it is rare. Both models learn the published estimate.

Challenges

Each one is checked on the settings you have tried.

  1. Scoring against the published estimate, with the name tag on, find a k where the flexible model beats the simple one on random rows but loses on whole countries.
  2. Score against the truth, and find settings where both models are further from the truth than the published estimate they learned from.
  3. Switch on the reform and see the published estimate's drop come out more than one and a half times the reform's true effect.
  4. On random rows, take the name tag away and watch the flexible model's error rise by more than half a point.
