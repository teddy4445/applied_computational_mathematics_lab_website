Simple math

Strains that share one town

Everyone is tracked by their immune history: which strains they have had. Both panels solve the same model in your browser.

Histories
new infections of strain j in history J = λⱼ × sⱼ,J × (share with history J)

Nobody catches a strain twice. After any other strain, susceptibility is 1 − σ (σ is the cross-immunity). Recovery adds the strain to the history; 10 days infectious.

Invasion
Rₑ(B) = R₀(B) × [never infected + (1 − σ) × had A]

A strain that lands with Rₑ below 1 cannot take off, however high its R₀.

Mutation and diversity
λⱼ = Σᵢ βᵢ Iᵢ Qᵢⱼ · N = exp(−Σ pₖ ln pₖ)

Qᵢⱼ is the chance a transmission by strain i founds strain j. N is the effective number of strains in the current mix.
