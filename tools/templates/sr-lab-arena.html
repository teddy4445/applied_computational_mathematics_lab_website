Simple math

Sixty throws, three searchers

The page simulates 60 throws (speeds 5–15 m/s, angles 0.2–1.2 rad, small measurement noise) from the law R = v²·sin(2θ)/g. The searchers see only v, θ and the ranges, and may use + − × ÷ sin cos and the number 1; every candidate gets a fitted scale and offset. Everything runs in your browser.

Random search
draw, fit, keep the best

Random trees of depth 1 to 5, as many as the budget allows.

Enumeration
smallest first, no repeats

Every different candidate in order of size; repeats (equal up to scale and offset) are skipped. One run: it has no randomness.

Genetic programming
select · cross · mutate · keep the best

200 formulas, tournament selection, subtree crossover and mutation, size limit 21.
