Simple math

One town, three observers

Lantern Bay is invented, so we know everything it really produced: about 1,666 million in its own currency. Each observer records a different part. The non-observed economy is what the statistics office misses.

Tax office
declared sales of registered firms

Sees new firms the register missed; never sees undeclared sales, informal or illegal production.

Statistics office
register + household survey

Surveys firms on its register, plus households for informal businesses and home-grown food. People under-report informal sales.

Card company
card payments only

Sees money move, not what was bought or whether it was declared. Undeclared sales are mostly paid in cash.
