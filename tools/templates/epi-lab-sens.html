Sensitivity and forecast scores

The module's SEIR town with a 60-day contact cut and seven uncertain inputs, and a simulated epidemic with two waves for backtesting forecasters. Everything runs in your browser.

Local
elasticity = % change in output ÷ % change in input

Cheap and clear, but it only describes the neighborhood of the baseline you pick.

Morris and Sobol
S1 = alone · ST = with all its interactions

Morris walks random paths to rank inputs; Sobol splits the output's variance among them.

Scoring forecasts
weighted interval score: width + penalties for misses

A proper score rewards intervals that are both sharp and right; coverage checks they mean what they say.

Challenges

Each one is checked on your current settings, in its own panel.

  1. Move the baseline cut start until its local effect on the reported peak drops below 0.5.
  2. Run Sobol with at least 1,024 samples and find an input that matters for the share ever infected almost only through interactions (first-order index under 0.05, total index at least 0.1 higher).
  3. Forecasts: stretch the trend's intervals until its 90% intervals cover 85–95% of the truths 14 days ahead.
  4. Forecasts: shrink the trend's intervals until they cover fewer than half the truths 7 days ahead, and see its score get worse with the same median.
