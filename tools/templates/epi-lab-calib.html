The module's SIR outbreak: a town of 100,000, beta 0.25 and gamma 0.1 (R0 2.5), 40% of infections reported with negative binomial noise. Everything runs in your browser.

Weighted least squares
loss = Σ (data − model)² / model

Each day's miss is divided by the noise expected on that day, so big days don't drown small ones.

Challenges

Each one is checked on your current settings, here or in the ABC panel below.

  1. Fit all 100 days with Nelder–Mead and get within 1% of the best fit.
  2. Find a start point from which gradient descent gets within 5% of the best fit.
  3. With 30 days of data or fewer, find a valley: fits within 5% of the best whose gamma values are more than 0.1 apart.
  4. In ABC, get the 90% interval of R0 narrower than 1.0 with at least 50 kept draws.
