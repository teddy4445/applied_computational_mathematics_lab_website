Simple math

One formula, many objects

Type a formula with + − * / ^, the functions sin, cos, exp, log, sqrt and abs, numbers, and variable names. The page turns it into a tree and measures it. Everything runs in your browser.

Trees and strings
(x − 2) × y → × − x 2 y

Prefix writes each operator first, postfix last. Neither needs parentheses.

Size, three ways
nodes · depth · weighted

Weights: + − × and leaves 1; ÷, √ and |·| 2; powers, sin, cos, exp and log 3. A graph with sharing stores repeated pieces once.

Units as types
[L] + [T] ✗

Sums need matching units; products add powers; sin, exp and log need a pure number inside. A free constant can take any unit.

Challenges

Each one is checked on your current formulas and settings.

  1. Make A and B different trees (different prefix strings) that the test calls the same function.
  2. Type a formula of your own (not a preset) whose graph with sharing saves at least four nodes over its tree.
  3. Build a formula A that contains a sine or cosine and whose units come out as a speed, L/T.
  4. Set the operator count to the smallest size at which fewer than 5% of formulas pass the unit check.
