Nine pendulums, one curve, one envelope

The page times nine simulated pendulums: Earth, Mars and the Moon (g = 9.81, 3.71, 1.62 m/s²), lengths 0.3, 0.6 and 1.2 m, swings from 0.1 to 2.5 rad, with 0.5% timing noise. The exact periods come from the elliptic integral. Everything runs in your browser.
