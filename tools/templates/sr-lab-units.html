Units as types
[T] = time · [g] = L/T² · [a] = L²

A drain-time formula must come out as a time. Products add powers; sums need equal units.

D · The ladder

Forty small tanks (levels 20–60 cm, holes 4–10 mm) are timed; the rungs then predict ten big ones (up to 2 m, holes up to 2 cm).

2%

Rung 0 fits a quadratic in level, hole and tank size (10 constants). Rung 1 fits a power law in the groups T·√(g/h), a/A and A/h². Rung 3 fixes the small-hole scaling and fits one constant.

Challenges

Each one is checked on your current settings.

  1. Write a drain-time formula that passes the units guard but is not Torricelli's shape: units are a filter, not a validation.
  2. Choose quantities that leave exactly two dimensionless groups, with the drain time T in one of them.
  3. Find readings where the plain cubic goes below empty, then pick the smallest soft weight (not hard) that keeps the lowest level above −1 cm and the fit inside under 2.5 mm.
  4. With at least 10% timing noise, show that rung 3 still recovers Cd within 3% of 0.6.
