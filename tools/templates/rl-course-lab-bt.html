Simple math

From “this one is better” to a score

People find it easier to compare two answers than to grade one. A Bradley–Terry model turns many comparisons into one score per answer.

The model
P(i ≻ j) = σ(rᵢ − rⱼ) = 1 / (1 + e^−(rᵢ − rⱼ))

Only differences of scores matter, so the scores are shown centred at 0.

The fit
maximise Σ log P(winner ≻ loser) − a small penalty on r²

A tie counts as half a win for each side. The small penalty keeps scores finite when one answer wins every time.

In RLHF
The fitted r is the reward model.

A policy is then trained to produce answers the reward model scores highly, usually with a KL term that keeps it close to where it started.
