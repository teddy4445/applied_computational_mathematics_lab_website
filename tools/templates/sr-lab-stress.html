A draining tank on the test bench

The page simulates a tank draining through a 1 cm hole. Torricelli’s law says the level falls at a rate proportional to its square root: dh/dt = −c·√h. A sensor reads the level every 2 seconds with the noise you choose and stops below 2 cm. You fit dh/dt = −c·h^p and test the fit the way Module 9 does. Everything runs in your browser.

The law and a power to find
dh/dt = −c·h^p, truth p = 1/2

Challenges

Each one is checked as you go.

  1. Find p within 0.01 of 1/2, with a 90% interval that contains 1/2, at 1 mm of noise or more.
  2. Check calibration twice: find a pipeline whose intervals contain the truth in fewer than half of the experiments, and one that reaches 85% or more.
  3. Run the negative control: with shuffled readings, see a log-log pipeline still report a power, while the trajectory fit’s error goes above 50 mm.
  4. Watch only the top of the tank (1 m to 60 cm), then falsify both rival laws with a single 60-second experiment.
