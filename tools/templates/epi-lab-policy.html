Lockdowns and vaccine priority

The module's made-up town: a million people, R₀ 2.5, a thousand hospital beds. Design one lockdown, then decide who gets a limited vaccine first. Everything runs in your browser.

A dial on transmission
β(t) = β₀ × (1 − u(t))

A lockdown of strength u between its start and end; 2% of infections need a bed for 10 days.

Two bills
output lost a day = 0.5 × u + 0.3 × caution

With caution on, people cut contacts by up to half as beds fill. Patients who find no bed die more often.

Vaccination as a flow
dS/dt = −λS − doses(t)

Doses go to susceptible people, group by group, in the order you choose; children, adults and seniors mix differently.
