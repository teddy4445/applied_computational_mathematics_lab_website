The forecast desk: a careful number, a year late

Thirty invented countries, quarter by quarter. A careful benchmark covers a year and is published a year after it ends; four quarterly indicators arrive at once (cash ratio, unemployment, digital payments). Three things happen along the way, each of which you can switch off: digital payments take over in half the countries, a recession, and an e-invoicing reform.
