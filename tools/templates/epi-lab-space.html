Simple math

Space in two ways

Five cities on a line, linked by travel; then 400 people on a contact network. Both run in your browser.

Cities
dIₚ/dt = β SₚIₚ/Nₚ − γ Iₚ + m Σ (neighbour I − Iₚ)

Each city runs SIR (γ = 1/10 per day); a share m of people moves to each neighbouring city per day, carrying their state.

Networks
T = τ / (τ + γ)

T is the chance an edge transmits before the infected end recovers. A large outbreak needs T × mean excess degree above 1.

Closure
m → m / 20 from the closure day

Cutting travel delays the wave in the far cities; it does not change how many get infected in the end.
