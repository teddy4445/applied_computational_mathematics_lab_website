Simple math

A surveillance system with a known truth

The course's simulated year: two waves in a town of 5 million; 1 in 4 infections becomes a case about 6 days later; reports arrive over two weeks. Everything runs in your browser.

Reporting triangle
N(t, d) = cases tested on day t, reported d days later

On day T you only see the cells with t + d < T: the newest test dates are incomplete.

Nowcast
nowcast(t) = arrived(t) ÷ F(T − t)

F(n): share of a day's cases reported within n days, estimated from complete test dates in a recent window.

Backtest
error = average |forecast − reported|

Rolling origins every 7 days: each forecast sees only reports up to its origin.
