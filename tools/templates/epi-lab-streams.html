Simple math

One hidden epidemic, three views

The module's simulated city: a million people, a lockdown on day 62, a relaxation on day 110. The infections (orange) are known; each panel shows what one data stream would see. Everything runs in your browser.

Reported cases
cases(t) = Σ share × infections(t − d) × delay(d)

Only a share of infections is reported, after a delay (here a gamma distribution).

Serosurvey
true ≈ (positive + sp − 1) ÷ (se + sp − 1)

Imperfect tests: false positives dominate when few people have been infected.

Wastewater
concentration = shedding ÷ flow × noise

Shedding from all recent infections, diluted on rainy days, measured with noise.

Challenges

Each one is checked on your current settings, in its own panel.

  1. Make reported cases fall for at least 10 days while infections rise.
  2. Serosurvey: with fewer than 1% ever infected, get a positive share more than 3 times the truth.
  3. Serosurvey: get the corrected 90% range narrower than 1 percentage point (with more than 0.5% infected).
  4. Wastewater: with noise of 0.5 or more, see the first wave turn at least 2 days before reported cases (and not before the infections).
