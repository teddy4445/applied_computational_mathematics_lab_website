Simple math

Learn the policy, not the values

Ten arms. Arm a pays a reward drawn around its hidden mean q*(a), with spread 1; the means are drawn around the reward offset.

Softmax policy
π(a) = e^H(a) / Σ_b e^H(b)

Preferences H start at 0, so every arm starts with probability 1/10.

Score-function update
H(a) ← H(a) + α (R − b)(1[a = A] − π(a))

Raise the arm you pulled if its reward beat the baseline b, lower the others, and the reverse if it did worse.

Why a baseline
b = R̄, the average reward so far

Any b leaves the expected gradient unchanged, but a good b cuts its variance. With b = 0 and rewards around +4, every pulled arm gets pushed up.
