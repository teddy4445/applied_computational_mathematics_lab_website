An inspection game, played by learners

A firm can comply or evade; the tax office can audit or not, and neither sees the other's move. Evading saves the tax (1); if audited, the firm pays the fine on top. Auditing costs the office c and recovers the tax. Both sides learn by trial and error. The payoffs are stylised: read the logic, not the levels.

Firms evade
x* = c / 2

just often enough that auditing still pays. The fine does not appear.

Challenges

Each one is checked on your current settings.

  1. With the fine doubling half way, find settings where long-run evasion ends within 1.5 points of where it started, while audits fall by more than a quarter.
  2. Bring long-run evasion below 4%. Which lever does it: the fine or the audit cost?
  3. Find a copying strength where the tipping loop is wider than 2 points of audit rate.
  4. Switch copying off and check that the two curves become one.
