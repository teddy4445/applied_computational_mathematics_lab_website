About the project

What this project is about

Using AI to uncover patterns, behaviors, and socio-economic impacts in the formal and informal economy.

This project examines how AI can help measure hidden economic activity, model institutional incentives, and analyze the behavior of street-level bureaucracies. Rather than treating the informal economy as a single scalar target, the work studies its structure, local variation, and policy sensitivity. The project mixes machine learning, deep learning, and agent-based reasoning to connect economic signals with institutional behavior and public outcomes.

Approach and outcomes

The methods the project relies on and what it has produced so far.

Methods

  • Machine learning on structured economic data
  • Deep learning for hidden-activity estimation
  • Agent-based simulation
  • Policy modeling
  • Behavioral analysis of public institutions
  • Counterfactual evaluation

Key outcomes

  • Models for estimating the size and local structure of unregistered economic activity
  • Behavioral analyses of incentives and prosociality in public institutions
  • Decision-support tools for policy design under institutional and social constraints

Timeline

How the project developed.

  1. 10/2023

    Project launched

    The lab formalized its hidden-economy and policy-simulation work into a single computational social-science track.

  2. 2024

    Unregistered-economy estimation

    Deep-learning and machine-learning pipelines were used to estimate non-observed economic activity from structured data.

  3. 2025-2026

    Institutional behavior studies

    The project expanded toward incentive design, emergency readiness, and geographic analysis of hidden economic behavior.
