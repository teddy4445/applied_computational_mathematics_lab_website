About the project

What this project is about

Leveraging biological principles and advanced analytics to evaluate scientific impact and trends.

This project studies the behavior of science itself through a computational lens. The lab borrows ideas from ecology, adaptation, and population dynamics to model publication behavior, collaboration structure, research excellence, and writing-style evolution. The aim is to move scientometrics beyond simple counts by building mechanistic and data-driven indicators that better reflect scholarly trajectories and field-level dynamics.
